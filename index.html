IBOG Rules & Café Mechanics

1. Objective: Compare rational numbers through binary operations to accumulate positive aroma points (Dark Roast) or negative points (Cold Brew).

2. Turns: Dark Roast (+) pieces move first, followed by Cold Brew (-) pieces in alternating turns.

3. Any Piece Mobility: All pieces on the board—whether starting from center or boxes 'a' and 'b'—can advance forward or move to adjacent valid tiles according to board connectivity and alternating tile color rules.

4. Rectangle Color Rule (Boxes a & b): A single rectangle cannot contain two pieces of the same color across boxes 'a' and 'b'. Once one box is occupied, the other is off-limits to pieces of that same color.

5. Infinity Pieces: Reaching infinity zones (+∞ ROAST DOMAIN or -∞ COLD DOMAIN) or yielding infinity converts a piece to ∞, allowing rook-like unobstructed movement across rows and columns.

6. Binary Operations: When opposing pieces meet in a rectangle (one in 'a', one in 'b'), a binary operation is triggered. Positive results credit Dark Roast, negative credit Cold Brew. Zero or indeterminate results eliminate both pieces.

7. Time Control: Each player is given 15 minutes. Running out of time deducts 25 points.

Official Recipe Notation Guide

Notation Format: [PieceValue]-[Row][Column][Box]

Example: -2-E7b indicates piece value -2 moved to row E, column 7, box 'b'.

Infinity Notation: ∞-K or ∞-A indicates conversion to infinity domain.

Operation Symbol: Appended with operation symbol if binary operation occurred (e.g., 0.25-F4a÷).
