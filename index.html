IBOG Rules & Café Mechanics

1. Objective: Compare rational numbers through binary operations to accumulate positive aroma points (Dark Roast) or negative points (Cold Brew).

2. Turns: Dark Roast (+) pieces move first, followed by Cold Brew (-) pieces in alternating turns.

3. Movement Rules: Pieces move forward or sideward to adjacent rectangles into box 'a' or box 'b'. Diagonal moves are NOT allowed. Pieces cannot move backward.

4. Rectangle Color Rule: A single rectangle cannot contain two pieces of the same color across boxes 'a' and 'b'. Once one box is occupied, the other is off-limits to pieces of that same color.

5. Infinity Pieces: Reaching infinity zones (+∞ ROAST DOMAIN or -∞ COLD DOMAIN) or yielding infinity converts a piece to ∞, allowing rook-like unobstructed movement across rows and columns.

6. Binary Operations: When opposing pieces meet in a rectangle, a binary operation is triggered. Positive results credit Dark Roast, negative credit Cold Brew. Zero or indeterminate results eliminate both pieces.

7. Time Control: Each player is given 15 minutes. Running out of time deducts 25 points.
